FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

What kind of businesses do you work with?

Mainly small and growing businesses that handle customers through Instagram DMs and email, such as online stores, studios and service providers. If repetitive messages eat your day, we can talk.

What can you automate on Instagram?

Instant replies, FAQs, pricing and availability answers, lead qualification, collecting customer and order information, follow-ups with people who go quiet, and handoff to a human.

Can automation handle orders?

It can collect order details, such as item, quantity and delivery information, and pass them to you or your tools in a clean format. Final decisions can stay with you.

Can a human take over a conversation?

Yes. Workflows are designed with handoff points, so you or your team can step in whenever a conversation needs a person.

What can you automate in Gmail?

Classifying emails, extracting key information, summarising threads, routing to the right person or tool, drafting AI-assisted replies and sending follow-ups.

Do I need to replace my existing tools?

Usually not. Workflows are built to connect to what you already use through APIs and integrations.

How long does an automation take to build?

It depends on the scope. A focused workflow is quicker than a multi-step system. After we talk through what you handle manually, I can give you an honest estimate.

Can you build a custom workflow?

Yes. If a process is repetitive and rule-based, or needs a bit of AI plus human review, it is a good candidate.
